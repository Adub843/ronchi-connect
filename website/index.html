Built for plant security

You stay in control the whole time.

Ronchi Connect is made for production environments, where an open door into a line PC is not acceptable.

  • You approve every person. Each technician is named on your screen and needs your Allow.
  • Always visible. A red bar stays on top of your screen for the entire session.
  • One click to stop. End session on the bar, or press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + X.
  • View only if you prefer. Let us watch without touching, or pause control at any time.
  • Encrypted end to end. Screen, input and files travel directly between the two computers.
  • No standing access. Codes work once. We cannot connect unless you open the app and share a new one.
  • Only Ronchi staff. A code is useless to anyone who is not a signed-in Ronchi technician.
  • Recorded. Every session is logged with who connected, when and what was noted.
